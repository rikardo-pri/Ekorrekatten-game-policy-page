Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-23

In short: Londonbörsen does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game operates entirely offline with no network access. No data leaves the device.

What is Londonbörsen?

Londonbörsen – to Billionaire is an economic board game. It runs locally on your device. There is no game server, account, or registration.

Data Collection

None. The app requires no login, account, or registration. No personal data is collected.

Network Traffic

The app does not communicate with any servers. All game data is stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools are used.

Children

The app does not collect data from any user, including children. The game is rated PEGI 3 / Everyone.

Contact
